# 15-minute check on a real phone

Automated tests cover the rules, the saves and the screens. They cannot feel the game, hear it,
or see a real ad. These are the things only a person with a phone can confirm. Install the APK
from the CI artifacts (`novalith-release-apk`).

## Feel (5 min)

- [ ] The piece follows the finger with no visible lag; releasing drops it instantly.
- [ ] Pieces fall, bounce slightly and settle. The pile does not jitter or creep when left alone.
- [ ] A merge "pops": flash of particles, ring, floating score.
- [ ] Dropping straight onto a twin shows PERFECT.
- [ ] A chain of merges shows COMBO and the screen shakes a little; effects never hide the board.
- [ ] Near the line: the dashed line turns red and pulses, the top of the screen glows red, and
      the last second plays in slow motion.
- [ ] Game over -> "Play again" -> the next piece can be dropped within about 2 seconds.
- [ ] Play 10 minutes: no stutter, phone does not get hot. (Debug build: bug button -> FPS.)

## Sound and vibration (3 min) - nobody has heard this audio yet

- [ ] Drop, impact, merge are pleasant and not too loud. Merge notes rise through a chain.
- [ ] Big merge (level 8+) sounds bigger. Game over sounds like an ending, not an error.
- [ ] Music loops without a click or a gap (listen across 32 seconds).
- [ ] Music / Sound / Vibration switches in Settings and in Pause work immediately.
- [ ] Vibration: light on drop, stronger on merge, strongest on big merge and game over.
- [ ] Phone call or switching apps: sound stops; coming back: music returns, game is paused.

If any sound is unpleasant, tell me which one: they are generated by `tool/gen_audio.py` and
are cheap to change.

## Text and languages (3 min)

- [ ] Settings -> Language: switch to Русский, Oʻzbekcha, 中文, हिन्दी, العربية, বাংলা, 日本語.
      Text appears in the right script (no empty boxes) and nothing is cut off.
- [ ] Arabic and Urdu: the layout is mirrored (back arrow on the right).
- [ ] Have a native speaker glance at any language you plan to promote: the translations were
      written without native review.

## Ads (3 min) - test ads only until production ids are set

- [ ] First launch in Europe (or with a VPN): Google's consent form appears before any ad.
- [ ] Game over with an ad loaded: "Continue" appears with a countdown; watching the ad clears
      the top of the board and the run goes on. It is offered once per run.
- [ ] Result screen: "Double coins" doubles the coins once.
- [ ] Close a rewarded ad early: no reward, no freeze.
- [ ] Airplane mode: no ad buttons, the game plays normally.
- [ ] After the third finished run an interstitial may appear between runs - never during play.

## Survival (1 min)

- [ ] Mid-run, swipe the app away from recents. Open it again: the menu says "Resume" and the
      run continues where it was.
- [ ] Rotate the phone: the game stays in portrait.
- [ ] Take two screenshots of the game and one of the menu for the store listing.
