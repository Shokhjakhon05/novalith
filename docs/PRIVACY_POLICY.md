# Novalith - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

Novalith ("the game") is a single-player, offline game. This policy explains what data is and is
not processed when you play.

## What the game itself stores

Your progress (score, coins, level, unlocked items, achievements, settings and an unfinished run)
is stored **only on your device**, in the game's private storage. The game has no account system
and no server of its own, and it does not send your progress anywhere. Uninstalling the game or
clearing its data removes this information. If device backup is enabled in Android, the system
may include the save file in your personal backup.

The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, location or microphone.

## Advertising

The game shows ads through **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK): optional rewarded ads that
you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between runs. To deliver and measure ads,
Google may collect and process device and usage data, such as the advertising ID, IP address
(which indicates approximate location), device model, OS version and how you interact with ads.

- How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent
through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice
at any time in **Settings -> Privacy options**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID
in the Android settings (Settings -> Google -> Ads).

## Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by **Google Play Billing**. The game receives only
the fact that the purchase exists; it never sees your payment details.

## Analytics

The released game contains no analytics SDK and does not collect gameplay statistics.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google
according to the content rating and settings configured for the game in Google Play.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
